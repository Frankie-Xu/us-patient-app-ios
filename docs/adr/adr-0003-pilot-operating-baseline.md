# ADR-0003: US primary-care pilot operating baseline

## Status and scope

**Proposed build freeze.** This ADR freezes the product and operating baseline for implementation and test fixtures. It does not constitute legal advice, a compliance certification, a BAA/DPA, or approval to process production PHI. The compliance/security owner and product owner must sign the applicable evidence before any production PHI launch. A missing signature or missing evidence is **No-Go**.

The baseline targets a US adult primary-care/family-medicine pilot. The iOS app is the patient channel. A short-lived, revocable clinician-reviewer link is the delivery channel. The accepted roles are the patient and an explicitly authorized clinical reviewer. Diagnosis, treatment, prescription recommendation and autonomous clinical decision-making remain out of scope.

## Frozen decisions and release evidence

Each row is a build constraint. “Go” means the implementation and evidence satisfy the row. “Pause” means the build may continue with synthetic/de-identified data while evidence or an owner is pending. “No-Go” means production PHI is prohibited.

## Operating rules

- The baseline is provider-neutral and implementation-ready; selecting vendors or changing regions requires a new decision record or an explicit amendment.
- Product facts, source-traceable AI review and doctor-view eligibility remain governed by [ADR-0001](adr-0001-api-contract-freeze.md) and the existing AI gate. This ADR does not authorize an AI-generated diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
- The production-boundary threat model and verification evidence in [ADR-0002](adr-0002-production-boundary-threat-model.md) remain mandatory. This ADR supplies the product defaults and operating values that ADR-0002 intentionally left open.
- The release-evidence manifest is an engineering check summary, not legal/compliance sign-off. A passing CI run never changes a No-Go into a Go.
- Any change to retention, share expiry, identity scope, local-cache behavior, telemetry retention, audit retention or key-rotation target requires product and compliance/security review before implementation.

## Consequences

The team can build a coherent pilot against stable defaults without selecting a vendor in domain code. The defaults make the online, short-retention, revocable-share path testable and keep full offline records out of the release. They also make legal and operational ownership explicit: unresolved entity, BAA/DPA, identity, deletion, recipient, telemetry or incident decisions stop production PHI even when the software tests pass.
